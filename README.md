## About Me

I'm a **Generative AI and Creative Technology** enthusiast from Rajasthan, India, studying B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at **Mewar University** (graduating 2027).

On the technical side, I build AI/ML and GenAI applications in **Python**, using **FastAPI**, the **Google Gemini API** with structured outputs, **scikit-learn** and **OpenCV**. On the creative side, I edit videos, study trends and tell short-form stories that people actually watch.

Most developers can't tell a story, and most creators can't build the tools. I do both, and that's the gap I'm interested in.

- 🎓 B.Tech CSE, Mewar University (2023 – 2027)
- 🧠 AI/ML industrial training at **CTTC Bhubaneswar**
- 🎬 Video editing internship at **iStudio**
- 📍 Chittorgarh, Rajasthan, India
- 🎯 Looking for **GenAI, AI content creation and creative technology internships**
