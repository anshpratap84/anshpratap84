## ✦ About Me

I'm **Ansh Pratap**, a final-year B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering student at Mewar University, Rajasthan, India (2027).

My interests live at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, software development, and digital creativity**.

I enjoy developing AI-powered applications with Python and Generative AI technologies, while also exploring video editing, visual storytelling, content strategy, and social media growth.

I believe technology is more than writing code, and creativity is more than making things look good. The interesting part is bringing both together.

- 🤖 Building AI/ML and Generative AI applications.
- 💻 Exploring Python, LLM integration, and intelligent automation.
- 🎬 Creating short-form videos and digital stories.
- 📈 Experimenting with content strategy, trends, and audience engagement.
- 🎨 Exploring creative design and AI-powered visual experiences.
- 🌱 Always learning, building, experimenting, and improving.

**My philosophy:** Build something useful. Make it meaningful. Tell its story.
